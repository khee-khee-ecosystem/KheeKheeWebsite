Discover the Right Creators

Finding the right creator is one of the most important parts of influencer marketing. Follower count alone doesn't tell you whether a creator is suitable for your campaign. You need to consider their niche, audience, location, language, engagement, content style, and budget.

Khee Khee helps you discover creators using the criteria that matter to your campaign.

Niche

Identify creators specific to your industry vertical and content category.

Location

Find regional and city-specific creators targeting geographic markets.

Audience

Evaluate follower demographics, age distribution, and interest overlap.

Language

Connect with vernacular, multilingual, and localized creator voices.

Engagement

Prioritize authentic interaction, comment depth, and real reach over vanity figures.

Budget

Discover creators that fit your commercial scope and pricing parameters.

Creator Category

Filter across micro influencers, regional creators, niche creators, or macro talents.

Campaign Requirements

Match content formats, posting schedules, licensing terms, and creative briefs.

Whether you are looking for micro influencers, regional creators, niche creators, or larger content creators, Khee Khee helps your team organize the discovery process and build a relevant creator shortlist.

Manage Creators Without the Spreadsheet Chaos

Spreadsheets can be useful for simple projects. But as your creator campaigns grow, spreadsheets can become difficult to maintain.

The Spreadsheet Chaos

Why Spreadsheets Break at Scale

When campaigns expand across dozens of influencers and multiple channels, static spreadsheets become an operational bottleneck:

  • ✕
    Creators change: Handles, rates, niches, and availability become outdated immediately.
  • ✕
    Deadlines move: Rescheduled posts create version control confusion and missed dates.
  • ✕
    Deliverables get updated: Revisions and multiple drafts get buried across email and chat threads.
  • ✕
    Campaign requirements evolve: Important brief updates fail to reach participating creators.
  • ✕
    Team members need access: Multiple colleagues overwrite data without permission control or audit trails.

Performance & Insights

Measure What Your Influencer Campaigns Deliver

Influencer marketing should not end when content is published. Your team needs to understand what happened after the campaign went live.

Depending on your campaign objectives, you may want to evaluate metrics such as:

Reach

Total impressions, audience exposure, and verified profile views generated across creator posts.

Engagement

Authentic likes, meaningful comments, saves, and story shares that signal genuine audience interest.

Content Performance

Analysis across formats (Reels, Stories, Carousels) to evaluate which creative styles resonance highest.

Creator Performance

Identify which individual creators drove the most impactful conversations and audience trust.

Campaign Activity

On-time publication rate, deliverable turnaround speed, and team execution efficiency.

Audience Response

Audience sentiment, product inquiries, click-through rates, and community feedback.

ROI-Related Outcomes

Attributable conversion value, promotional code redemptions, and overall return on campaign spend.

Khee Khee helps bring campaign management and performance visibility into the same workflow. Use campaign insights to identify which creators, content strategies, and approaches are working—and use those learnings to improve future campaigns.
