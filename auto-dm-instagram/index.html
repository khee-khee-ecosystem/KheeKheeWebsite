What Is Instagram DM Automation?

Instagram DM automation is a way to automatically send a configured direct message after a specific Instagram interaction.

With Khee Khee, creators can connect their Instagram content with automated DMs triggered by Reel comments or Story replies. Instead of manually checking comments, verifying followers, replying to Story messages, and sending the same link repeatedly, creators can automate these repetitive steps.

This makes automated Instagram messaging useful for creators who regularly use Reels and Stories to distribute resources, rewards, offers, links, and exclusive content.
