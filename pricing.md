## Overview

Khee Khee operates two transparent pricing models tailored to distinct personas in the creator ecosystem:
1. **Creator Automation Plans (B2C / Solo Creators & Power Creators)**: Flat monthly subscription tiers for official Meta-verified Instagram comment-to-DM automation, story replies, follower gating, and lead generation.
2. **Agency & Brand Marketplace Plans (B2B / Agencies & Teams)**: Per-seat tiered subscriptions for creator discovery, campaign workflows, chat collaboration, brief management, and shared per-seat DM automation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can creators use Khee Khee completely free?
Yes. The Creator Free Plan costs ₹0 forever and includes 800 DM credits, 2,000 comment reply credits, 1 connected Instagram account, and 1 active automation rule per month. No credit card is required to sign up.

### Are there free trials for paid plans?
Yes:
- Creator paid plans (Starter, Pro, Growth) come with a **7-day free trial**.
- Agency Marketplace plans (Business, Enterprise) include a **10-day free trial**.

### What happens if I exceed my DM credits?
If you exhaust your monthly allocation, automations pause safely without dropping Instagram connections. You can either purchase instant Top-Up packs (e.g., 1,000 DMs for ₹149) or upgrade to a higher tier.

### Is Instagram DM Automation safe and compliant?
Yes. Khee Khee connects exclusively through official Meta Graph APIs with OAuth 2.0 verification. It never asks for, handles, or stores your Instagram password, ensuring 100% compliance with Meta developer policies.
