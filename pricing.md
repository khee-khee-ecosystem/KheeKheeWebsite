# Pricing — Khee Khee Creator Marketing Operating System

## Overview
Khee Khee offers flexible pricing plans tailored for Creators, Brands, and Agencies. All plans include access to core campaign management tools, real-time messaging, and verified profile management.

## 1. Creator Plan
- **Target Audience**: Content Creators, Micro-influencers, Talent
- **Price**: Free ($0/month)
- **Features Included**:
  - Profile Creation & Media Kit builder
  - Direct campaign discovery from verified brands & agencies
  - Campaign task tracking & status updates
  - Performance analytics & engagement reporting
  - Standard email and in-app support

## 2. Brand Plan
- **Target Audience**: Direct-to-Consumer Brands, E-commerce, Local Businesses
- **Price**: Starter Tier & Growth Tier (Custom options available)
  - **Starter**: $49/month — Up to 5 active campaigns, discovery database search, direct messaging.
  - **Growth**: $149/month — Unlimited active campaigns, advanced creator search filters, ROAS tracking & exportable reports.
- **Features Included**:
  - Hyperlocal & niche creator discovery
  - Direct workflow collaboration & content approvals
  - Real-time conversion tracking & campaign ROAS metrics
  - Priority support

## 3. Agency Enterprise Plan
- **Target Audience**: Influencer Agencies, Talent Management Firms, Enterprise Marketing Teams
- **Price**: Custom Enterprise — Contact sales (`info@kheekhee.com` / `support@kheekhee.com`)
- **Features Included**:
  - Full Agency CRM & Creator Roster management
  - Multi-brand client management & permissions
  - Automated white-label campaign reporting
  - Dedicated account manager & custom integrations
  - Priority SLA support & custom onboarding
